# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Calgary home buyers and sellers researching who to trust with the biggest transaction of their lives. They arrive from referrals, Instagram, or Google, usually on a phone, often in the evening. Segments: first-time buyers, move-up buyers, downsizers, investors, and newcomers to Calgary. Their job to be done: decide within a minute or two that Chan Kawaguchi is credible, likable, and worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

Living With Chan is the personal marketing site of Chan Kawaguchi, a REMAX Complete Realty agent in Calgary, Alberta. It exists to convert visitors into leads (contact form, buyer/seller intake, phone calls) by making Chan feel like the warm, established local expert she is. Success = more qualified inquiries, not awards for their own sake; the elevated craft is in service of trust.

## Brand Personality

Warm, expert, personal. Chan (she/her) is the approachable neighborhood expert, not a faceless luxury brokerage. The site should feel like a beautifully produced property film narrated by someone you'd happily have coffee with: cinematic and confident in presentation, human and generous in tone.

## Anti-references

- Trendy-gimmick portfolios: neon palettes, glitch effects, cursor-chasing blobs, anything that will look dated in a year.
- Style over substance: effects must never bury listings, contact info, or lead forms; every animation supports comprehension or emotion, never delays the CTA.
- Cold big-brokerage templates and generic IDX real estate sites.

## Design Principles

1. **Cinematic, then useful.** One breathtaking opening moment (the 3D hero); after that, motion recedes and content leads. The deeper the scroll, the calmer the page.
2. **Warmth is the luxury.** Premium here means warm bronze light, generous whitespace, and slow confident easing, not darkness or exclusivity for its own sake.
3. **Chan is the product.** Her face, voice, and local knowledge outrank any effect. If a design choice competes with her, cut it.
4. **Performance is credibility.** All Three.js/GSAP code lazy-loads; the page is readable before any WebGL boots; `prefers-reduced-motion` gets a complete, dignified experience.
5. **Every scroll earns something.** Scroll-driven animation must reveal information (a stat, a listing, a step), never just decorate distance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` fully: all GSAP/Three.js motion collapses to static or simple fades; content is never gated behind animation.
- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on all text, including text over the 3D hero.
- All interactive elements keyboard-reachable with visible focus; 44px minimum touch targets (mobile-heavy audience).
- The site must remain fully functional with WebGL unavailable (fallback hero imagery).
