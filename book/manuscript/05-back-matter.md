# Ten Things I Would Tell My Younger Self

*(and every woman reading this)*

1. **You are not behind.** There is no schedule. There is only your river and your crossing.

2. **Boredom is a compass, not a character flaw.** If success is putting you to sleep, you climbed the wrong mountain. Climb down and pick another. That is navigation, not failure.

3. **Being the only woman in the room is a position of strength.** You will be remembered, you will be underestimated, and both are useful.

4. **Money is the most replaceable seat at the table.** Trust, diligence, judgment, and care are the scarce ones. You already own them.

5. **Ask "teach me, I'm listening" early and often.** Every year you delay humility, you pay full tuition for lessons someone would have given you for coffee.

6. **Put every agreement in writing, especially with people you love.** Paper is not distrust. Paper is kindness with a long memory.

7. **Never trade your name for a partnership's peace.** The moment you must become smaller to keep something, it has already cost more than it can return.

8. **Your pain is raw material.** You do not have to be grateful for it. You only have to refuse to waste it.

9. **Do it scared.** Confidence is not the entry requirement. It is the graduation gift. Motion creates evidence, and evidence kills fear.

10. **Home is not a place. It is the people.** Build for them, with them, and you will never once wonder whether the work was worth it.

# Your Journal: All the Questions in One Place

Use these pages slowly. One honest answer is worth more than a finished workbook.

**The Crossing**

- What "river" are you standing in front of right now?
- What is one choice you could renew every morning for the next year?
- Who has quietly sponsored you in your life, and what did it teach you about accepting help?
- What was your lemonade stand, the moment you created value out of nothing?
- What skills do you use weekly that you have never given yourself professional credit for?
- Who are your people, the ones you are really building for?
- What belief about money did you inherit, and is it serving you?

**Against the Current**

- Where are you succeeding at something that bores you?
- What rule were you raised to follow that you have secretly outgrown?
- What is, or was, your falling-apart season, and what question is it asking you?
- Complete the sentence: "I do not want to survive. I want to..."
- Of the six seats (money, credit, knowledge, time and effort, opportunity, trust), which two can you fill today?
- Write your imaginary line: "People like me don't..." Then name one woman who did it anyway.
- What would the ability to say no change in your life, specifically?
- If life paused you for six months, what income would keep arriving?

**Stronger Together**

- Who have you been dismissing by their cover, and what might they know that you need?
- When were you last underestimated, and what did it actually hand you?
- What is your invisible resume: five things you managed, negotiated, saved, or rebuilt that never appeared on a CV?
- What is one sentence that states your strength without apology?
- Who are three women you could build something with, and what small thing could you attempt first?
- Have you ever stayed small to keep the peace? What did it cost?

**Your Turn**

- What does your real inventory look like at your current age, and who would you rather bet on?
- Whose sacrifice does your beginning honour?
- Which of the six moves comes first, and on what date?
- What is your one small brave thing this month, and who will you tell today?
- Write the letter you hope to send one year from now. Date it. Keep it.
