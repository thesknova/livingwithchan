# Against the Current — Book Project

A motivational gift book for women aged 30-60, built from Chan's own Instagram storytelling (@living.with.chan). It serves as a brand-building and lead-generation tool for Living With Chan: her true story from refugee camp to real estate freedom, with lessons on finding your strengths and the power of partnership.

## Sources

Every fact and most signature phrases come from Chan's Instagram posts and bio (captions pulled July 2026): the "came from nothing" reels, the university reel, the parents/legacy reels, the "never judge a book" reel, the Meet Chan intro post, and the Orchid Tap Bar Canada Day post. Signature lines kept verbatim: "I did not want to survive, I wanted to live," "home is not a place, it's the people," "being different doesn't make you limited," "I loved being the stone in the current."

1. **Arrival year:** one post says sponsored to Canada in **1980**, another says the government brought the family in **1979**. Manuscript uses 1980 (journey beginning earlier). Confirm.
2. **Years investing:** bio says **25+ years**; intro post says "over the last 30 years." Manuscript uses "more than twenty-five years." Confirm.
3. **Parents' health crisis timing** relative to the first deal at 21: the manuscript places the crisis before/around the start of her entrepreneurship journey, per the reel. Confirm sequence.
4. **First deal details** (age 21, detached home in Calgary, no own money or credit): the manuscript deliberately avoids inventing deal mechanics. Chan may want to add the real structure as a sidebar.
5. **Shaganappi lemonade stand**: manuscript says the family "lived near" the golf course. Confirm.
6. **The escape from Laos**: written impressionistically through her parents' memories since Chan was very young. She should add or correct any details she wants told.
