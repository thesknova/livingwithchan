# "Against the Current" — Marketing & Launch Plan

How to use the book to build the Living With Chan brand and generate real estate leads among women aged 30-60.

---

## 1. Positioning

- **What it is:** A short motivational gift book (roughly 15,000 words, reads in about 90 minutes) telling Chan's true story: refugee camp to financial freedom, framed as a guide for women starting over at 30, 40, 50+.
- **What it is NOT positioned as:** a real estate textbook or a sales brochure. The brand-building happens through the story; the "About Chan / Work with Chan" page does the conversion quietly at the end.
- **One-line pitch:** "From a refugee camp to financial freedom: proof that it is not too late for you."
- **Audience:** Women 30-60. Sub-segments: starting over (divorce/layoff), empty nesters planning retirement, aspiring first-time investors, women who feel "behind."

## 2. Formats (in priority order)

1. **PDF lead magnet** on livingwithchan.com/book: free download in exchange for name + email. This is the highest-leverage format; every download is a lead.
2. **Print gift book** (5x8", softcover, approx. 120 pages): closing gift for female clients, giveaway at women's events, Orchid Tap Bar counter copies. Print via KDP paperback or a local Calgary printer for small runs.
3. **Amazon KDP listing** (paperback + Kindle at a nominal price): the purpose is credibility ("author" status, Amazon reviews), not royalties.
4. **Audiobook (later):** Chan narrating even just the Letter and Epilogue as Instagram audio content is high-value; a full audiobook can wait.

## 3. Funnel

Instagram reel (story chapter) → link in bio → /book landing page → free PDF via email capture → 6-email nurture sequence (one lesson per email, each ending with a soft CTA) → invitation to a 15-minute call / DM "HOME" → client or coaching lead.

Notes:
- The existing site runs Formspree (50 submissions/month free tier). A book funnel will exceed that quickly; plan to add a proper email tool (e.g., MailerLite/ConvertKit free tiers) for the download + nurture sequence.
- Suggested nurture sequence (maps to book parts): 1) The river / you are not behind. 2) Your lemonade stand (hidden skills). 3) The six seats at the table. 4) It's not too late math. 5) One small brave thing challenge. 6) Invitation to work with Chan.

## 5. Offline distribution

- **Closing gifts:** every female buyer/seller gets a signed copy. Cost per unit ~$4-6 printed; far more memorable than wine.
- **Events:** women's networking groups (Calgary has several), REMAX office events, The Sat Market booth, Orchid Tap Bar pop-ups (book + beverage cross-promotion writes itself: "lemonade and the lemonade-stand book").
- **Referral engine:** the last page tells readers to pass the book on. Print a "This book was given to me by ______" line inside the front cover with Chan's contact block beneath it, so every passed-along copy stays a lead generator.
- **Speaking:** the book is a credential. Pitch Chan to Calgary women-in-business groups, libraries, and immigrant-entrepreneur programs as "author of Against the Current."

## 6. Brand rules for production (from Chan's standing preferences)

- The official **REMAX Complete Realty logo** (not a text substitute) must appear on the back cover / final page of any printed or PDF edition, alongside the Living With Chan logo (`Pictures/living_w_chan_logo.jpg`).
- **No em dashes** anywhere in the text (the manuscript already complies).
- Brand palette for cover and interior accents: charcoal `#3A3937`, near-black `#111111`, warm bronze `#A3856F`, cream `#F7F7F7`, sand `#E3E0D8`. Font: Inter for interior; a serif display face is acceptable for the cover title.
- Always she/her for Chan.

## 7. Cover concepts (for a designer or Canva session)

1. **The Current (recommended):** deep charcoal background, a single bronze line drawing of a woman swimming diagonally against flowing horizontal lines, title in large cream serif. Elegant, gift-book feel, no photo needed.
2. **Lemonade Through the Fence:** close crop of a paper cup of lemonade passed through chain-link, warm golden light. Emotive and literal; pairs perfectly with the Orchid Tap Bar cross-promo.
3. **Portrait cover:** Chan's professional headshot (bronze blazer look), charcoal band top and bottom, title overlaid. Strongest for personal-brand recognition; best if the book leads with "local Calgary author."

Back cover: 3-sentence hook, one pull quote ("I did not want to survive. I wanted to live."), Chan's headshot thumbnail, contact block, REMAX Complete Realty + Living With Chan logos.

## 8. Launch sequence (suggested)

1. Chan reviews and corrects the manuscript facts (see README "Facts to verify").
2. Professional copyedit (or a careful second pass) + layout.
3. Cover design in Canva/designer using a concept above.
4. Build /book landing page on livingwithchan.com + email capture.
5. Soft launch: free PDF announced to existing followers, ask first 20 readers for testimonials.
6. KDP publish with those testimonials as editorial reviews.
7. Begin the 12-week reel serialization.
8. Print run for closing gifts and events.

## 9. Success metrics

- PDF downloads (leads) per month
- Email open/reply rate on the nurture sequence
- DMs with the keyword "HOME" or "BOOK"
- Consult calls booked attributable to the book
- Copies gifted at closings (relationship metric, drives referrals)
