## Strategy Overview

The ad targets people already interested in real estate investing and leads with the
one question every investor asks: does the property actually cash flow? The landing
page has a live cash-flow calculator, so the ad promise ("let's run the numbers")
is delivered within one click. That message match is the core of the campaign.

Calgary's fundamentals do the selling: near-record-low vacancy, no PST, no provincial
land transfer tax, and entry prices far below Vancouver/Toronto. The ad copy uses
these as proof points rather than hype.

**Important — Meta Special Ad Category:** Real estate ads must be declared under the
**Housing** special ad category. This disables age, gender, and postal-code targeting
and replaces lookalikes with Special Ad Audiences. Targeting below is written within
those constraints. Skipping the declaration risks ad rejection or account restriction.

> I help investors find properties where the numbers make sense, and we run the cash-flow analysis together before you write an offer. Try the free calculator on my site.
